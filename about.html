We are NEXORA.AI

We are a group of students who came together for a hackathon to create Nexora.AI, an AI and HTML-driven website designed to improve employability. Our platform helps job seekers develop their skills, improve their CVs, and prepare for employment, while also helping companies identify and connect with candidates who are suitable for specific job opportunities. Our goal is to bridge the gap between talented individuals and employers through the power of AI and technology.
